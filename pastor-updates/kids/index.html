A Place for Families

HungryGen Spokane Kids Ministry

Our mission is to partner with parents to raise a generation of children who know God intimately, stand firmly on the truth of His Word, and walk in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our mission is to lead them beyond surface-level faith into a deep, personal relationship with Jesus—equipping them to live holy lives, serve in ministry, and function as active members of the Body of Christ, both now and in the future.

“Let the little children come to me, and don’t hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. Truly I tell you, anyone who will not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”Mark 10:14–15

Age Groups

Needs confirmation: these four groups are copied from Tri-Cities

Toddlers

12 months – 2 years
3 years / sensory

This is a loving, Spirit-filled environment where little ones are introduced to God’s love through worship, simple prayers, Bible stories, sensory-based activities, and caring volunteers. They begin to experience God’s love and learn that church is a joyful place.

PreK / Kinder

4 – 5 years

Children learn foundational Bible truths through worship, stories, prayer, and hands-on activities. They discover that God is real, He loves them, and He speaks to them.

1st – 3rd Grade

6 – 10 years

This group focuses on building a personal relationship with Jesus and growing in the power of the Holy Spirit. Through worship, Scripture memory, skits, and Bible lessons, children learn to pray, hear God’s voice, and live out their faith daily.

Pre-Teens

4th – 5th Grade
11 – 12 years

Pre-teens are trained as young disciples of Jesus. At this stage, teaching becomes deeper and more practical, helping them understand biblical doctrine and how to live it out daily.

Our goal is to prepare them for youth ministry and life as bold, Spirit-led followers of Jesus.
