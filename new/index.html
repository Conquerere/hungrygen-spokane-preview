Your first Sunday

What to expect

Tap a card to see more. You do not need to know anything ahead of time.

1WorshipExtended, Spirit-led worship to open the night.

We begin with passionate, extended worship led by our worship team. Sing along, lift your hands, or simply take it in. There is no pressure to do anything you are not comfortable with.

2PreachingA Bible-centered message you can live out this week.

Each message comes straight from Scripture, with practical teaching that calls us to follow Jesus wholeheartedly. Bring a Bible or follow along on your phone.

3Prayer & ministryTime to respond, receive prayer, and encounter God.

Services often close with ministry time, an open invitation to respond to God and receive prayer. You are welcome to come forward or stay in your seat. If you want someone to pray with you, just ask.

Want more prayer? Friday Prayer Night is every Friday at 8:00 PM.

4What to wearThere’s no dress code. Come as you are.

There’s no dress code at HungryGen Spokane. Come as you are. Whether that’s jeans and a hoodie or something dressier, you’ll fit right in, and we’re just glad you’re here.

Good questions

First-visit FAQs

What time is church on Sunday?

Sunday Service is every Sunday at 6:00 PM. See the Sunday Service event page for full details. Whether you are new to faith, in a hard season, or looking for a church home, that is a good place to begin.

Where do you meet?

If you have questions before you come, call (509) 818-0560.

  • Address115 E Pacific Ave #209, Spokane, WA 99202
  • Phone(509) 818-0560
  • ParkingFree on-site lot, plus free street parking around the building
Do I need to be a Christian to come?

No. Everyone is welcome, and you do not need to have everything figured out to walk through the door. If you are new to faith, curious, or looking for a church home, come as you are.

Is there something for my kids?

Yes. Kids Ministry meets during the Sunday 6:00 PM service. See the Kids page for current details.

Is there something for teens?

Yes. Students connect through Connect Groups rather than a traditional youth service.

How do I request prayer?

We would be glad to pray with you.

  • FridaysFriday Prayer Night at 8:00 PM
  • Phone(509) 818-0560
  • Emailinfo@hungrygenspk.com
  • Share a requesthungrygen.com/needprayer
Do you have other campuses?

Yes. HungryGen has campuses in the Tri-Cities, Everett, Renton, Spokane, and Yakima, Washington, as well as Portland, Oregon, and Sacramento, California.
