Connect Groups at HungryGen Spokane

Life is better together. Connect Groups are a simple way to grow in Jesus with people from this church in Spokane. Youth at HungryGen Spokane also meet in Connect Groups rather than a separate youth service.

Browse the groups below and request to join on Church Center. Groups may meet on different days and times. If you have questions, talk with us on Sunday.
