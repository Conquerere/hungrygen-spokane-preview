Campus pastors

Meet Bryson and Priscilla

Bryson and Priscilla Still are the campus pastors of HungryGen Spokane.

As the campus pastors of HungryGen Spokane, we are passionate about seeing lives transformed through the power of Jesus Christ. Our mission is to disciple and raise up believers who are not only devoted to Jesus but are also called to be kingdom ambassadors, impacting their communities with the love and truth of the gospel. We are committed to seeing nothing less than city transformation in the name of Jesus. We believe that Jesus is the only way and that by seeking first His kingdom, everything else in life will fall into place. Our heart is to raise a generation of passionate followers who are completely sold out for Jesus, living with purpose and dedication as they advance His kingdom on earth.

— Pastor Bryson & Priscilla

Questions people often ask

Where do you meet?

HungryGen Spokane meets at Spokane Valley Baptist Church, 1222 S McDonald Rd, Spokane Valley, WA 99216. Sunday service and Friday Prayer Night are here. Get directions.

When is Sunday service?

Sunday service is every Sunday at 6:00 PM.

Is there something for kids on Sunday?

Yes. We have Kids Ministry.

What else happens during the week?

Friday Prayer Night is every Friday at 8:00 PM at Spokane Valley Baptist Church. Youth meet in Connect Groups: Simon's group for guys and Bella's group for girls.

How can my teen get connected?

Youth meet in Connect Groups: Simon's group for guys and Bella's group for girls. Sign up on Church Center, or talk to Simon or Bella for more info.

How do I get in touch?

Email info@hungrygenspk.com or call (509) 818-0560.
