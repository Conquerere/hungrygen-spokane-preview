Discipleship Track

Discipleship Path

Discipleship Track 101 | Spring 2027

For the believer who wants to learn fundamental Christian principles and how to live life to glorify Jesus.

  • Mondays 6:00–7:30 PM
  • 7 weeks
  • Free
  • In person
More about Discipleship Track

This 7-week crash course is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the Christian faith. It is engaging, interactive, practical, and free of charge, and it is only for local, in-person members of HungryGen Spokane.

  • Schedule: Meets weekly on Mondays from 6:00–7:30 PM
  • Season: Spring 2027
  • Leaders: Malika and Rebecca

Coming soon

The first session date is coming soon. Request to join on Church Center to get details when they’re announced.

At the end of Discipleship Track 1, students are encouraged to register for Encounter Weekend, a 3-day retreat with personalized sessions of inner healing, deliverance, and ministry time.

Water Baptism

Ready to be baptized?

Baptism is an important act of obedience, a public declaration of faith, and a celebration of the new life we have in Christ. We baptize by full immersion, following the pattern of the New Testament.
